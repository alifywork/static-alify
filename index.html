Most engagements kick off within two to three weeks of signing. The first week is always a paid discovery sprint so we agree scope against reality, not assumptions — after that, build starts the following Monday.

Fixed price against a written scope for defined projects; a monthly retained team for open-ended product work. Discovery is priced separately and credited against the build if you continue. You'll never see an unquoted line item.
